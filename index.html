7Data Deletion

To request deletion of information that Exarplay may hold about you, email xrexarplay@gmail.com with the subject “MG University VR data deletion”. Include a description of the information or account concerned and enough detail for us to locate it. Do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents. We may ask for proportionate information to verify and process your request.

We will review and respond to requests and, where applicable, delete information we control unless retention is required or permitted by law. Data controlled by Meta or another service provider may need to be managed through that provider's own account or privacy tools. The actual data sources, completion timeline, exceptions, and any user-facing in-App deletion controls must be confirmed before publication; this email procedure must be monitored and operational.
